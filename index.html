HOW TO PLAY PANEL DE PON

Released in 1995 for the Super Famicom (SNES), Panel de Pon (localized in the West as Tetris Attack) is a fast-paced fairy action puzzle game starring Lip, the Fairy of Flowers.

🎮 Core Rules & Progressive VS Battle:
  • Use the 2-tile white cursor to swap two horizontal panels (you can also swap a panel with an empty space!).
  • Line up 3 or more matching panels horizontally or vertically to clear them.
  • Active Skill Chains: While matched panels are flashing and popping, keep moving and slide panels underneath falling columns to trigger huge chains!
  • Progressive VS Ladder: Defeat each Fairy & Boss across 12+ escalating stages (and infinite Overlord loops beyond Stage 12!). Higher-tier CPUs actively dig through garbage, raise their own stack when low, and set up multi-chains!
  • Thick Chain Garbage: 4+ Combos send wide 1-row slabs; 2x+ Chains send thick multi-row blocks where clearing adjacent matches peels off the bottom layer!
⌨️ 1P / 2P Keyboard, Gamepad & Touch:
• 1P Default Keys: W A S D / Arrows + Space / Z Swap + L-Shift / X Raise
• 2P Default Keys: I J K L / Numpad + Enter / / Swap + R-Shift / . Raise
• Controllers (1P & 2P): Plug in USB/Bluetooth gamepads — D-Pad/Stick moves, A/B/X swaps, L/R raises!
